Sproutfolio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Sproutfolio helps you identify your houseplants, remember to water them and find out what is wrong when they struggle. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your rights.

Who is responsible

Mathias Ødegaard Andersen, Denmark MathiasCodingClub@gmail.com

What we collect and why

Data Why Legal basis (GDPR)
Account ID. The app creates an anonymous account the first time you open it. To store your plants and keep them separate from other users' plants. Contract (providing the app)
Apple ID email address, only if you choose Sign in with Apple. Apple may give us a private relay address instead. To keep your plants when you change phone. Contract
Your plants: names, species, location you describe, pot and soil, notes, care guide, watering history, soil checks, diagnoses and their results. To run the app: watering plans, reminders, history and diagnoses. Contract

Subscription status (which plan, when it renews or expires). We never see your card details. To unlock the app for subscribers. Contract
Usage of AI features: how many identifications and diagnoses you run, and their cost. To keep the service affordable and stop misuse (daily limits). Legitimate interest
Whether a diagnosis helped (yes / partly / no), if you answer. To measure and improve how often diagnoses are right. Legitimate interest

We do not collect your location, contacts or browsing history, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Watering reminders and the home-screen widget are created and shown on your phone only.

AI analysis (Claude by Anthropic)

To identify a plant or diagnose a problem, the app sends the photo, your description and the plant's details and history to Claude, an AI service from Anthropic PBC (USA), via our server. The app asks for your permission before the first time this happens, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy. Without it, identification and diagnosis are unavailable; everything else still works.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, the data is not used to train Anthropic's AI models. Anthropic may keep it for a limited period for safety and misuse monitoring, as described in its own privacy policy.

AI answers can be wrong. Diagnoses are suggestions, not certainties, and always tell you to check the soil and roots yourself.

How long we keep data

We keep your plants, photos and history for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, they are deleted from our servers. Records of AI usage are kept without a link to you after deletion, for cost accounting.

Photos are stored privately; only you can see them through the app.

Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Delete your account and all your data in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent to AI analysis in Settings → Privacy.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing MathiasCodingClub@gmail.com.
  • Complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, www.datatilsynet.dk).

Cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel it.

Children

Sproutfolio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each user can only access their own data (row level security), photos are in private storage, and the AI service keys never leave our server.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
